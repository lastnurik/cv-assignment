Grad-CAM can highlight the hand, face or background. It shows sensitivity to image regions; it does not establish why a prediction is correct.

A small, limited dataset

We compared ConvNeXt with the A1 CNN and MobileNetV2, and tested the effect of ImageNet pretraining. The data covers one participant in one indoor setting. No synthetic training images were used.
